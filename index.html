Direction Signals

A two-timeframe direction filter built from completed 5-minute and 25-minute candles. Signals appear only when both timeframes agree.

CALL BIAS

Both timeframes trend up and RSI is above 50.

PUT BIAS

Both timeframes trend down and RSI is below 50.
